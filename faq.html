FAQ

Where can I park?
Parking details for your event is available when you RSVP.
Are kids allowed?
Yes! Children are a blessing and we would love to have them celebrate with us!
What should I wear?
Dress code varies by event — details are shown for each event once you RSVP, and on the Agenda page.
Can I bring a plus-one?
Only if your invitation includes additional guests — your RSVP will show exactly how many you're able to bring.
When do I need to RSVP by?
Please RSVP by October 31, 2026.
What's the dietary situation? Is the food halal?
Yes, the food is halal. If you have any allergy restrictions, please reach out to us directly.
What if I can't find my name when I try to RSVP?
Please reach out to us — contact information can be found below.
Can I change my RSVP after I've submitted it?
Yes, feel free to update or change your RSVP — just RSVP again and the system will log your changes :)
When does the nikkah ceremony start?
We strongly advise guests to start arriving at 6 PM. The nikkah ceremony will start at 6:30 PM sharp, and no exceptions will be made for friends or family.
